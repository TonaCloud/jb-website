About Me

A normal person learning AI in public — from Germany, with curiosity.

Who I am

I'm not a developer, data scientist, or tech professional. I work in something completely different. But in early 2025, I had a conversation with Claude about business ideas that changed how I looked at everything — and I decided I couldn't keep watching from the outside.

This site is the result of that decision. It's my public notebook: what I learn, what breaks, what surprises me, and what I think it all means for people who aren't part of the tech world.

Based in Germany. Writing primarily in Spanish but switching to English when it feels right. Learning AI without a computer science degree, without a technical background, without any special advantage — just curiosity and time.

Why I do this in public

Because most content about AI is either too technical or too hype-driven. There's very little written by regular people, in real time, about what it actually feels like to go from zero to using these tools day to day.

I want to be that voice. Not because I'm an expert — precisely because I'm not.

What you'll find here

  • News — short takes on AI developments that I find interesting or relevant for non-experts
  • Posts — longer entries documenting real experiments: building this website, comparing AI tools, figuring out what's actually useful vs. overhyped
  • Honest failures alongside the wins — no polished success stories, just what actually happened

Tools I use

My current stack, updated as it evolves:

  • Claude (Anthropic) — for writing, reasoning, and building things I don't know how to build
  • ChatGPT — for quick questions and when I want a second opinion
  • Perplexity — for searching anything that requires current information
  • Cloudflare Pages — hosting this site, for free
  • Proton Mail — email on the custom domain
  • GitHub — version control, even if I barely understand git
